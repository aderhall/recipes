1-1.5h, serves 5-8

Sautée on medium:

- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2-3 large onions, diced
- [ ] 3 celery ribs, diced
- [ ] 2 large carrots, peeled and chopped

Add for shorter duration:

- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1.5 tbsp cumin
- [ ] Chili in some form (ideally homemade chili powder, ~2 tbsp)

Deglaze with:

- [ ] 1/2 cup red wine
- [ ] 6 cups veg broth (or water + crumble in a stock cube)
- [ ] 5 15oz cans black beans

Bring to boil, simmer uncovered 30min on med low.

Optionally puree about 1.5 cups of solid, 2 cups of liquid.

Thicken with:

- [ ] 1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp water slurry. If it doesn't work, repeat once

Season:

- [ ] 2 tbsp lime juice
- [ ] Salt + pepper to taste
