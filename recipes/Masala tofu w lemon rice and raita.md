# Lemon rice
- Cook rice
- [ ] 1 cup jasmine rice
- Fluff and add:
- [ ] 2 tsp neutral oil
- [ ] 1/2 tsp turmeric
- [ ] juice of 1 lemon
- [ ] 1/4 cup roasted cashews, roughly chopped
- [ ] big handful cilantro, chopped
- [ ] 1/4 cup shredded coconut
- [ ] salt + pepper

# Tofu
- Soak tofu in salted boiling water for 10 mins, then dry on a towel
- [ ] 1 block extra firm tofu
- [ ] 2 cups water + 1 tbsp kosher salt
- Coat with
- [ ] 2 tsp garam masala
- [ ] 3 tbsp cornstarch
- [ ] salt + pepper
- Shallow-fry in neutral oil until golden brown and crispy (approx. 8-11 min)

# Raita
- Mix:
- [ ] 8oz chopped cherry tomatoes or cucumber
- [ ] 1/2 serrano or jalapeno
- [ ] 1 cup yogurt
- [ ] salt + pepper
- [ ] lemon juice
