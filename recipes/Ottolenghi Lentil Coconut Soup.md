Serves: 8

Origin: Ottolenghi

Sautée on medium-high ~8min to caramelize:

- [x] 4 tbsp veg oil
- [ ] 2 medium onions, peeled and finely diced
Add and carefully stir for ~2min:
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh ginger, peeled and finely chopped
- [x] 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- [ ] 2 tbsp curry powder
Add and stir for ~1min:
- [ ] 10oz (300g) red lentils, rinsed and drained
Add and leave to heat:
- [ ] 28oz (800g) canned chopped tomatoes
- [x] 5 cups cold water
- [x] 2 tsp salt, 2 "generous grinds" pepper
Homogenize coconut milk and set aside 8 tsp for garnish. Add the rest and bring to a boil, then simmer on med for ~25min, until lentils soft but still hold shape:
- [ ] 28oz (800g) coconut milk
Garnish (also with remaining coconut milk):
- [ ] 1/4 cup cilantro or parsley
- [ ] Lime wedges, to serve

Steps:

1. Heat oil in medium saucepan on medium-high heat, sautée onion for 8 min, stirring often, until soft & caramelized.
2. Add curry powder, chili flakes, garlic & ginger, cook 2 more mins, stirring continually. Add lentils, stir for a minute, then add tomatoes, (50g cilantro stalks?), 5 cups cold water, 2 tsp salt, 2 generous grinds pepper, leave to heat.
3. Gently whisk coconut milk in a bowl. Set aside 8 tsp for serving, then put rest in soup. Bring to boil. Turn heat down to med and leave to simmer gently for 25 min, until lentils are soft but still hold shape.
4. Serve 8, with coconut milk, cilantro? leaves and lime wedges as garnish.
