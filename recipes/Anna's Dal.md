Red lentil dal / soup / curry

Onion (I do one medium for about 3 portions)

garlic

ginger

garam masala (maybe 3x the amount of the other spices)

ground cumin

ground corriander

ground cardamom

chili powder

corriander seeds (I just do maybe 1/2 tsp)

cumin seeds (I just do maybe 1/2 tsp)

red lentils

chopped tomatoes blended (I like it without pieces of tomato but with the extra fibrousness compared to passata)

coconut milk

(really just garam masala would be sufficient probably, then add any Indianish spices you want. see if your curry powder has spices similar to garam masala if you don't have it, try to approximate)

Fry a finely chopped onion in vegetable oil in a saucepan (probably not nonstick).

Add garlic, ginger, spices. (I make onion and garlic semi-crispy, ginger not really crispy)

Add red lentils.

Add tomato and coconut milk, aiming for a final consistency that is right for eating on flatbread-- not soupy enough that you need a spoon. (I do maybe more volume of coconut milk than tomato but idk).

Simmer until lentils cook. Don't let it stick to the bottom, add more tomato or coconut milk if needed.

Make flatbread in meantime:

Flour, bread flour might be better but plain is fine

salt

veg oil

water

salted butter (optional)

Large frying pan, probably should be nonstick

Something to roll with, a bottle is good

Mix flour, salt, tsp-tbsp vegetable oil, and warm water to make a dough. Volume of water should be about half that of flour. (warm might not do anything)

Add flour until it's not sticky, knead a bit if you feel like it.

Get a big frying pan, nonstick probably best.

Dust surface, break off a piece of dough that will fit in your pan when rolled to a 0.5 cm thickness.

Heat pan on high while you roll out the first one.

Roll out on a flour-dusted surface then put in pan, it shouldn't be sticking to the surface or the pan, but don't put it in the pan really floury as the flour will burn.

Flip when one side is done in a few min. It may puff up a bit it may not.

Roll the next one out

Put a piece of salted butter on it while it's still hot, let it melt a bit and spread it all over on both sides.
