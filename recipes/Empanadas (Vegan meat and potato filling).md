# Filling:

Boil until tender enough to eat comfortably:

- [ ] About 3 small waxy potatoes, cut into 1/2 inch cubes

Heat to shimmering temp in a pan on med high heat:

- [ ] ~2tbsp olive oil

Fry in olive oil until you would want to eat it:

- [ ] 1 lb beyond meat brick or alternative, defrosted and mashed up

Add and fry until aromatic:

- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 1 tbsp chilli powder (homemade is best)
- [ ] 2 tsp cumin
- [ ] ~4 cloves garlic, minced or crushed

Deglaze with:

- [ ] 1/2 cup red wine

Crumble up over pan and then mix in:

- [ ] 1 vegetable stock cube

Add:

- [ ] 1/2 cup water

Mix in potatoes: filling is ready.

# Empanadas

Make masa from:

- [ ] 4 cups masa harina (eg maseca) according to directions on packaging

Place a ~3-4 tbsp ball of masa on a ziploc atop a hard, flat surface. Place another ziploc on top and smush the ball between the ziplocs using a frying pan or other heavy flat object. You should make ~5 inch diameter corn tortillas about 3-4 mm thick (experiment and see what works for you).

Put ~ 2-3 tbsp of filling in the center of an uncooked tortilla, fold it over to make a semicircular pouch and seal the fold by pressing together.

Heat on medium high in a large, high-walled pan:

- [ ] Neutral oil, deep enough to cover half the empanada (you will flip them to get both sides)

Test oil temp by dipping a corner of an uncooked empanada in: it should sizzle enthusiastically. When oil is hot enough, fry the empanadas, flipping gently with tongs when after ~2 minutes once the bottom face is golden and starting to brown.

Serve hot with salsa.
