# Chili tomato paste

Toast in a dry hot pan (med heat) until fragrant:

- [ ] 2 dried anchos, deseeded
- [ ] 4 dried pasillas, deseeded
- [ ] 2 tbsp cumin seeds
- [ ] Optionally, some good smoked paprika

Transfer to food processor along with:

- [ ] 2 tbsp chipotle in adobo sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 4-8 cloves of garlic, peeled and bases trimmed

Pulse until combined into a fairly smooth paste

# Chili

Fry to desired cookedness:

- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] Beyond meat brick or alternative, defrosted and mashed with a fork

Then add chili paste (above) and cook until tomato paste starts to brown and/or stick to bottom of pan. Deglaze with:

- [ ] 1/4 cup red wine

Add:

- [ ] Stock cube (crumbled up)
- [ ] 45oz canned (black/pinto/mixture) beans, rinsed and drained
- [ ] 2 cups water
- [ ] 1-3 bay leaves

Simmer for 30 min.
Season and garnish:

- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] Pepper to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp cilantro / parsley, chopped
- [ ] 1-2 tsp Lime juice, sherry vinegar or red wine vinegar
- [ ] Tortilla chips, sour cream, grated cheddar
