1-1.5h, serves 4-7. All veggies other than onions are optional, spices are entirely a suggestion.

If using potatoes or carrots, start bringing a pot of water to a boil (just enough to cover hard veggies).

Fry on med-high in pan until slightly translucent:

- [ ] Neutral oil
- [ ] 2-4 medium onions, diced

Add to the boiling water and cook vigorously until slightly less cooked than you want (take a bit out and taste every so often to check):

- [ ] 2-3 carrots, chopped
- [ ] 1-2 lbs potatoes, cubed

Add and coat onion:

- [ ] 1 tsp - 4 tbsp curry paste of choice (taste for spiciness)

Reduce heat if anything is cooking too vigorously. Add oil if necessary and slightly sear:

- [ ] 1-4 heads broccoli stems, chopped, leaving florettes aside

Fry with onions and curry paste for 1-2 min:

- [ ] 2-4 cloves garlic, finely minced
- [ ] 2-4 tbsp ginger, finely minced

Add and fry for ~30 sec:

- [ ] 3-4 tbsp curry powder, max 1 tsp cumin (optional)

Quench with:

- [ ] 2-3 small cans coconut milk

Stir and bring to a mid-grade simmer. Cook for ~10min, then add:

- [ ] 1-2 cans chickpeas or lentils
- [ ] 1 can baby corn
- [ ] ~45oz tofu (cubed)
- [ ] Potatoes and/or carrots if using, when they are sufficiently softened/parboiled

Cook for 5-7 minutes until all is warm and edible texture.

Season to taste and serve over rice.
