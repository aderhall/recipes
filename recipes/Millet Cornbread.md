- [ ] Unsalted butter (for greasing pan)
- [ ] 300g (2 cup) masa harina
- [ ] 140g (1 cup) millet flour
- [ ] 90g (1/2 cup) potato starch
- [ ] 60g (1/2 cup) tapioca flour aka tapioca starch
- [ ] 4 tsp baking powder
- [ ] 12 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- [ ] 4 large eggs
- [ ] 3 cups whole milk
- [ ] 1.5 tsp sea salt
- [ ] 4 tbsp granulated sugar

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Butter a large baking pan.
2. In a large bowl, mix masa harina, millet flour, potato + tapioca starch, and baking powder. Make a well in center.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together melted butter, eggs, milk, salt and sugar. Pour into the well in the dry mixture and mix until just blended.
4. Pour into pan and bake for 25 minutes, until knife comes out clean. Cool pan on rack for 10 mins, cut and serve.
Wrap for storage up to 3 days.
