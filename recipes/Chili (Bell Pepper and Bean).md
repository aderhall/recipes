Fry to desired cookedness:

- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] 1 med onion, diced
- [ ] 1 lg red bell pepper, chopped
- [ ] 2 med carrots, chopped
- [ ] 2 ribs celery, chopped
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt

Then add and cook:

- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 2 tbsp chili powder
- [ ] 2 tsp cumin
- [ ] 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano

Quench with:

- [ ] 28-30oz canned diced tomatoes with juice
- [ ] 45oz canned (black/pinto/mixture) beans, rinsed and drained
- [ ] 2 cups veg broth or water

Add:

- [ ] 1 bay leaf

Simmer for 30 min.
Season and garnish:

- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] Pepper to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp cilantro / parsley, chopped
- [ ] 1-2 tsp Lime juice, sherry vinegar or red wine vinegar
- [ ] Tortilla chips, sour cream, grated cheddar
