40min, serves 2-3

Fry until translucent:

- [ ] Olive or neutral oil
- [ ] 1 med onion, diced

(Optionally toast the rice now)
Fry for 1-2 mins until cooking:

- [ ] 2-4 cloves garlic, minced

Fry for 30s:

- [ ] 1 tsp cumin

Quench with:

- [ ] 2 cups water (or whatever the rice bag says for 1 cup)
- [ ] 1 can black beans (rinsed)
- [ ] 1-2 tsp salt, or a veg stock cube

Bring to boil, then add (unless you toasted it):

- [ ] 1 cup rice

Simmer covered on low for ~20mins until rice absorbs water.
Season (optional):

- [ ] Cilantro or parsley
