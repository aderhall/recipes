2h, serves 3-5

Oven to ~425 generally (400 is cooler, 450 is quite hot)

Chop desired vegetables, put in a bowl and coat with a generous amount of olive oil. Salt lightly and add desired herbs and spices.

Vegetable, approximate timescale of cooking, seasonings:

- [ ] Broccoli, 30min
- [ ] Waxy potatoes, 1hr
	- [ ] Paprika, cinnamon, chili powder, cayenne
	- [ ] Rosemary or thyme
- [ ] Sweet potatoes, 1hr+, cumin & chili powder
- [ ] Garlic (peeled cloves or entire head), 1hr
- [ ] Carrots, 1hr, warm spices or thyme
- [ ] Onions chopped into rings, 1hr
- [ ] Kale, 30min (toss after halfway), cumin & chili powder, finish with lemon

Check on vegetables periodically (timers!)
