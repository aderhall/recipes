1-1.5h, serves 4-6

Fry until translucent/browning:

- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] 2 onions, diced

Add and bring to boil:

- [ ] 5-8 cups water or veg broth

Add to water and boil until desired texture:

- [ ] 2-3 carrots, diced
- [ ] Waxy potatoes, cubed
- [ ] 2 tomatoes, chopped into random chunks

Optionally add:

- [ ] 1/3 cup white wine

Add and set timer for 10 mins:

- [ ] 2-4 cups brassica (eg tuscan kale or savoy cabbage), cut in ribbons

Off heat and add:

- [ ] ~4 cloves garlic, chopped
- [ ] Red pepper flakes if desired
- [ ] ~2/3 cup frozen peas
- [ ] Extra water if too thick
- [ ] Salt (a sizeable amount) and pepper, to taste

If you didn't use wine:

- [ ] Splash (~1 tbsp) white balsamic vinegar
